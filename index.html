about.

a pond seen from above. koi swim by flocking, come to a pointer that holds still, scatter from one that moves fast, and gather where you tap.

with the camera on they follow your hand instead. a video uploaded works the same way, and either can show through the pond as a faint background.

keys: s settings, h hand, space pause, r reseed, g style, esc close.
